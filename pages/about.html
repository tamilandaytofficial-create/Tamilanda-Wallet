About Tamilanda Wallet

Tamilanda Wallet is a private personal and business money management wallet designed to help you track your daily money, accounts, income, expenses, buyers, EMIs and pending payments in one place.

What You Can Manage

  • 🏦 Bank, cash and UPI accounts
  • 💰 Income and expenses
  • 👤 FF buyers and receivables
  • 🤝 Money you need to give
  • 📅 Your own EMIs
  • 🔄 Recurring payments
  • 📊 Reports and calculations
  • 💾 Backup and restore

Privacy First 🔐

Tamilanda Wallet currently works locally on your device. Advanced security features such as PIN lock, biometric unlock and encrypted backups can be added in future versions.
